The Strategy

Solutions for Senior Audiences (Age 61 median)

1. Visual Hierarchy & Scannability

  • Break up text blocks → short, scannable sections with icons

  • Higher contrast and strategic whitespace

2. Strategic Visuals

  • Trust badges (licensed doctor, privacy-certified, secure payment)
  • Product imagery or benefit icons
  • Testimonials or success rate callouts
  • Progress indicators ("Step 1 of 2")

3. Brand Alignment

  • Pull color palette and typography from the quiz
  • Create cohesive journey (reduces friction)
  • Medical aesthetic reinforces professionalism
